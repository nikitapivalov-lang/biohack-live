# Histology lesson sources

## Learning material

- Кузнецов С. Л., Мушкамбаров Н. Н. Гистология, цитология и эмбриология. 3-е издание, 2016. Общая гистология, темы 7–12; мышечные ткани, с. 182–197; нервная ткань, с. 198 и далее.
- User-provided «Поясни за гисту», tissue sections; skeletal contraction, PDF pp. 107–108, and cardiac muscle, p. 117. Used for comparison, with original EGE-level explanations and diagrams.
- [Studarium: epithelium](https://studarium.ru/article/77), [connective tissues](https://studarium.ru/article/78), [muscle](https://studarium.ru/article/79), [nervous tissue](https://studarium.ru/article/80).
- [Reactome: Smooth Muscle Contraction](https://www.reactome.org/content/detail/R-HSA-445355) and [Physiology, Smooth Muscle](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK526125/) for the optional calcium–calmodulin–MLCK pathway.

The primary lesson stays at EGE level. Molecular regulation, germ-layer exceptions and myelin details are in closed-by-default «Углублённо» sections. The standalone anatomy lesson «Ткани человека» now contains main, epithelium-simple, epithelium-stratified, epithelium-glandular, connective, atlas, skeletal and smooth. Ontogenesis links to it; legacy ontogenesis tissue routes redirect to their canonical standalone routes. The separate epithelial screens use original vector illustrations for six single-layer variants, three common stratified epithelia, two rarer stratified types in optional depth, and a separate glandular-function illustration. Classification, locations and functions were checked against Kuznetsov/Mushkambarov, sections 7.2–7.4 (especially pp.123–127). Ciliation and brush borders are specializations rather than new layer classes.

- Skeletal: both actin lengths stay at 200 model units; the thick filament and A band stay at 220 units. Z-lines approach while overlap increases, then return for the schematic relaxation.

## Connective tissue atlas

New standalone screen: `#lesson/tissues/connective`. Thirteen illustrated adult tissue types/variants are arranged in five groups. Embryonic and rare specialized tissues are outside this EGE-oriented atlas. Compact and spongy are explicitly described as organizations of bone, not separate tissue families. Basic information is synthesized from Kuznetsov/Mushkambarov, 2016, topics 8–10 (pp.133–181); OpenStax section 4.3 was used for cross-checking. The diagrams are original BioHack SVGs and adapt to dark mode; photographs preserve their staining and full composition.

Twelve photographs were added; the existing white-adipose photograph is reused. Selected images use Commons preview sizes (500–1400 px wide), re-encoded to JPEG without cropping. Brown-adipose has a smaller original resolution. The spongy-bone image retains both ordinary and polarized-light panels and its original scale bar. Screen zoom is not a calibrated microscope magnification.

The lymph card deliberately uses a **normal lymph-node preparation**, not a supposed lymph smear. Its title, caption, recognition notes and modal identify the organ, while a separate original diagram explains lymph inside a vessel. The distinction between tissue, cell and organ is explicit.

## Revised overview drawings

The connective overview now depicts nine fibroblasts, two macrophages, bundled collagen fibrils and thinner branching elastic fibers within ground substance. The detailed loose-connective scheme uses the same richer original illustration with independent clip IDs; dense regular and irregular schemes each show eight distinct spindle-shaped fibroblasts. The nervous overview shows a branching multipolar neuron, a segmented myelinated axon, terminals, two astrocytes and a capillary. Astrocytic branches contact portions of the soma/dendrites and vessel; they are not depicted as a continuous myelin sheath. Labels and explanatory cards distinguish astrocytes from oligodendrocytes and Schwann cells. All drawings are original SVGs with reversible dark-theme colors.

Anatomical cross-check: [Neuroglial Cells, Neuroscience (Purves et al.)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10869/), [The Astrocyte: Powerhouse and Recycling Center](https://cshperspectives.cshlp.org/content/7/12/a020396.full).

## Muscle morphology drawings (2026-10-25)

Original SVGs in the existing tissue palette; morphological reference: S. L. Kuznetsov, N. N. Mushkambarov, *Histology, Cytology and Embryology* (2016), topic 11: pp. 184–188 (skeletal muscle), 193–194 (cardiomyocytes and intercalated discs), 195–196 (smooth myocytes). Skeletal fibers show multiple peripheral nuclei, aligned transverse striation and no branching. Cardiac tissue shows a branching chain of separate striated cells, central nuclei and thicker intercalated discs. Smooth muscle shows spindle-shaped cells, one elongated central nucleus per cell and no transverse striation. The overview and the three comparison cards use original schematic illustrations, not reproduced textbook images; all dimensions and visible cell counts are illustrative. Each card links to the existing attributed micrograph in the atlas.

## Interactive skeletal muscle dissection (2026-10-25)

Interaction reference: https://lab.neofamily.ru/muscle-contraction/ (progressive exploration from whole muscle to contractile structures). All BioHack drawings and implementation are original. Starts directly with an isolated muscle. Six levels: muscle, transverse cut, fascicle, multinucleated muscle fiber, myofibril, sarcomere. Connective sheaths and hierarchy follow Kuznetsov & Mushkambarov (2016), pp. 184–190, especially 186 (endo-/peri-/epimysium), 188–190 (sarcomeres, actin/myosin, sliding filaments and ATP-dependent cross-bridge cycle). Filament lengths and A-band stay constant during the shortened teaching cycle; relaxation shows recovery of length under external forces, not active pushing by filaments. Drawings are schematic, not to scale.
